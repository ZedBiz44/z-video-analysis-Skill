# Analysis Patterns

Use only the sections needed for the request.

## Quick Summary

- **Answer:** one short practical conclusion.
- **Source and coverage:** title, link, access date, and portion inspected.
- **Useful points:** three to seven findings.
- **Important visual proof:** only material visible evidence.
- **Claims or uncertainty:** what was not proved.
- **Next action:** only when useful.

## Tutorial or Procedure

- **Outcome:** what the process accomplishes.
- **Prerequisites:** account, access, tools, or source material shown or confirmed.
- **Procedure:** actions in the demonstrated order, with exact visible labels when readable.
- **Timestamps:** beside the relevant step.
- **Warnings or gaps:** skipped steps, outdated screens, unclear settings, or claims not demonstrated.
- **Completion check:** the visible result that proves the procedure worked.

Do not copy a dangerous, destructive, credential-exposing, or unauthorized instruction merely because a presenter demonstrates it.

## Focused Question

- Answer the question first.
- Cite the relevant timestamp or page state.
- Explain the spoken and visible support.
- State what remains uncertain.
- Avoid summarizing unrelated parts of the video.

## Research or Decision Brief

- **Recommendation:** one practical choice.
- **Video findings:** directly observed or heard.
- **Presenter claims:** statements not independently proved.
- **Checked elsewhere:** current outside facts with sources.
- **Business fit:** why the recommendation fits the stated use.
- **Risk and next test:** the smallest safe validation step.

## Companion Handoff

End the analysis with a short labeled packet when another skill must continue. Do not paste the full analysis when a compact source, purpose, facts, and constraints packet is enough.
