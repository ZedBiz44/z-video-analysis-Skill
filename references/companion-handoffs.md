# Companion Handoffs

Analysis, critique, and graphic production are separate jobs. A handoff does not expand the requester's authority.

## Handoff to Z Video Critique

Use `z-video-critique` when the requester asks whether a video is creatively ready, what creative problems should be fixed, why a strong video's creative execution works, or how two videos compare creatively.

Finish the information analysis first when both jobs are requested. Send:

- the actual complete playable video or public source URL;
- intended use, audience, and desired response;
- analysis coverage and important timestamps;
- verified source facts and presenter claims kept separate;
- approved brief, copy, brand references, and platform when supplied;
- elements that must remain unchanged; and
- the exact critique question.

Do not provide a critique verdict on behalf of the companion. The critique result owns its creative verdict, material findings, protected elements, and done-when conditions.

If critique is unavailable, state:

**Information analysis completed; ZedBiz video critique companion unavailable.**

## Handoff to Z Graphic Production

Use `z-graphic-production` only when the requester asks to create or edit a still asset based on the analysis, such as a thumbnail, diagram, comparison graphic, social image, or slide visual.

Send:

- intended asset and use;
- audience and desired response;
- approved dimensions or channel;
- exact approved copy;
- verified facts from the analysis;
- authorized source images, frames, logos, and brand references;
- visual direction supported by the request; and
- elements that must remain unchanged.

Do not send presenter claims as approved advertising claims. Do not treat an extracted video frame as licensed marketing artwork merely because it was available for analysis.

If graphic production is unavailable, state:

**Video findings prepared; ZedBiz graphic production companion unavailable.**

## Combined Request

For “analyze this video, critique it, and make a thumbnail”:

- analysis establishes the information, source facts, claims, and useful moments;
- critique independently judges the video's creative execution;
- graphic production receives the approved brief and creates the requested still asset; and
- no skill publishes, delivers externally, spends additional money, or changes approved creative direction without the required authority.
